A Four-Antenna GNSS Deployment at Haitian Yizhou 在杭州湾跨海大桥海天一洲部署四天线 GNSS 实验设备

Created on November 19, 2026

2026   ·   GNSS-IR   sea-level monitoring   field deployment   ·   research-projects

On November 19, 2026, my labmate Ruizhe Lü and I installed a four-antenna GNSS system at Haitian Yizhou on the Hangzhou Bay Bridge. The installation gives us a site to test GNSS-based sea-surface-height retrieval using several antenna configurations.

2026 年 11 月 19 日，我和师弟吕睿喆来到杭州湾跨海大桥的海天一洲，安装了一套四天线 GNSS 实验设备，用于开展海面高反演实验。

Both upper antennas are right-hand circularly polarized (RHCP). One has a cast-aluminum ground plane intended to suppress reflected signals. We pair this antenna with the lower left-hand circularly polarized (LHCP) antenna for dual-antenna processing. The lower RHCP antenna and the remaining channels also let us explore other observation combinations.

上方两副均为右旋圆极化（RHCP）天线。其中一副加装铸铝底壳，用于抑制反射信号，并与下方的左旋圆极化（LHCP）天线配合开展双天线解算实验。下方另一副右旋天线及其余观测通道也将用于探索不同的观测组合。

Special thanks to StarPak (星火北斗) for providing custom equipment for this experiment. I also thank Ruizhe Lü for working alongside me on the deployment, and the site crew for helping assemble and install the mounting bracket.

特别感谢 星火北斗公司（StarPak） 为本次实验提供定制设备。也感谢师弟吕睿喆一起完成现场部署，以及工人师傅协助搭建和安装支架。

I’ll close with the view we had at the end of the day: sunset over Haitian Yizhou.
