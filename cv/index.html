Selected Research and Open-Source Projects 代表性科研与开源项目

ERA5-SH Global Scale-Height Dataset

Reproducible global dataset and processing workflow for tropospheric-parameter scale heights derived from ERA5 profiles.

  • Estimated ZTD, ZHD, ZWD, Tm, PWV, and WVD scale heights and validated them with 591 radiosonde stations.
  • Published as a first-author Scientific Data paper; data released on Zenodo with 2,873 combined downloads as of July 9, 2026.

GMR-Water

Open-source MATLAB software for multi-GNSS, multi-frequency, and multi-observation GNSS-MR water-level retrieval.

  • Supports SNR, carrier-phase, and pseudorange strategies, batch processing, quality control, visualization, and accuracy evaluation.
  • Published as a second-author GPS Solutions software paper.
