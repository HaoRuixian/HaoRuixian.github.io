Professional Summary 个人简介

Ruixian Hao works on geoscience applications of low-cost GNSS receivers, high-precision GNSS-IR/GNSS-MR algorithms, and key tropospheric parameters for GNSS meteorology and atmospheric remote sensing. His recent work includes the ERA5-SH global scale-height dataset and GMR-Water, a MATLAB software package for multi-GNSS water-level retrieval.

Projects 项目经历

  • ERA5-SH Dataset

    Global gridded scale-height dataset for ZTD, ZHD, ZWD, Tm, PWV, and WVD derived from ERA5 reanalysis profiles.

    • Built exponential-fitting workflows for tropospheric-parameter scale heights.
    • Validated products with global radiosonde-derived scale heights.
    • Dataset released on Zenodo in two parts with 2,873 combined downloads as of 2026-07-09.
  • GMR-Water

    MATLAB software for GNSS-MR water-level retrieval with multi-GNSS, multi-frequency, and multi-observation processing.

    • Supports SNR, carrier-phase, and pseudo-range retrieval strategies.
    • Provides GUI, quality control, visualization, and accuracy-evaluation workflows.
  • Water Vapor Scale Height in China Climate Zones

    Analysis of water vapor scale-height variation and driving factors across Chinese climate zones.

    • Used radiosonde observations from 89 stations during 2016-2019.
    • Quantified spatial-temporal patterns and potential impacts on GNSS water vapor modeling.
