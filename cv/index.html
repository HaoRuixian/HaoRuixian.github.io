Professional Summary

Ruixian Hao is a graduate student at the College of Geoscience and Surveying Engineering, China University of Mining and Technology - Beijing. His research focuses on GNSS meteorology, tropospheric delay modeling, atmospheric water vapor, GNSS water vapor tomography, and GNSS reflectometry for water-level and sea-surface-height retrieval.

Projects

  • ERA5-SH: Scale-height dataset for tropospheric parameters

    Global gridded scale-height dataset based on ERA5 pressure-level reanalysis profiles.

    • Calculated vertical profiles for ZTD, ZWD, ZHD, Tm, PWV, and WVD from ERA5 reanalysis data.
    • Estimated scale heights through exponential fitting and evaluated product accuracy with 591 global radiosonde stations from 2022.
    • Published as a Scientific Data paper and released supporting code on GitHub.
  • GMR-Water: GNSS-MR water-level retrieval software

    MATLAB software for water-level retrieval using GNSS multipath reflectometry.

    • Integrated spectral analysis, inverse modeling, carrier/pseudo-range combinations, and multi-frequency fusion methods.
    • Supports SNR, carrier phase, and pseudo-range based retrieval with visualization and accuracy evaluation.
    • Provides GUI and scripted workflows for reproducible and batch processing.
  • Spatial-temporal variation of water vapor scale height in China

    Analysis of water vapor scale-height characteristics and impact factors across Chinese climate zones.

    • Used radiosonde observations from 89 Chinese stations to calculate water vapor scale height from 2016 to 2019.
    • Analyzed spatial-temporal variation patterns and impact factors across climate regions.
    • Published in Advances in Space Research.
  • RTGS: RealTimeGNSS Studio

    Python-based project for practical real-time GNSS workflows.

    • Supports workflow-oriented GNSS processing experiments.
    • Complements research projects with practical real-time data handling tools.
