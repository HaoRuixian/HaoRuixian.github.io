## Presenting a real-time low-cost GNSS-IR system

My presentation focused on applying low-cost GNSS hardware to real-time GNSS Interferometric Reflectometry (GNSS-IR) sea-level monitoring. I introduced our work on real-time data acquisition, edge-computing platform development, wireless transmission, and continuous sea-level retrieval with low-cost multi-GNSS receivers.

As affordable high-precision GNSS receivers continue to improve, their role is expanding beyond conventional positioning. They now provide a practical foundation for distributed environmental sensing, coastal monitoring, and other long-term observation tasks. The presentation allowed me to discuss both the engineering details of our system and the broader question of how low-cost GNSS equipment can support reliable real-time monitoring. Feedback from participants also gave me useful ideas for improving the observation platform and processing algorithms.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

## 汇报低成本实时 GNSS-IR 系统

本次报告主要围绕低成本 GNSS 设备在实时 GNSS 干涉反射（GNSS-IR）海（水）面高监测中的应用展开。我介绍了我们在实时数据采集、边缘计算平台搭建、无线数据传输以及连续海（水）面高反演等方面的探索与实践。

近年来，随着低成本高精度 GNSS 接收机性能不断提升，它们的用途正在从传统定位逐步扩展到分布式环境感知、海岸带监测和长期连续观测。借助这次报告，我不仅介绍了系统的技术实现，也与参会专家讨论了低成本 GNSS 设备在可靠实时监测中的应用潜力。报告后的反馈为进一步改进观测平台和数据处理算法提供了新的思路。

</div>

## Learning from the GNSS community

CPGNSS Forum brought together researchers from universities, research institutes, and industry. The technical program covered precise positioning, GNSS remote sensing, PPP, RTK, integrated navigation, and BeiDou applications. I was impressed by both the academic standard of the meeting and the quality of its organization. The keynote talks, technical sessions, and informal discussions offered a valuable overview of current research trends and emerging directions in GNSS.

Beyond the formal sessions, the forum was also a welcome opportunity to reconnect with friends and colleagues. I met my friend Wu Xuanyu, who is studying in Nanjing, and we discussed our recent research progress and academic experiences. I also had productive conversations with several young researchers from Chang'an University about GNSS-IR, water-level monitoring, low-cost GNSS systems, and real-time processing algorithms.

Face-to-face conversations with researchers working on similar problems often reveal new perspectives that are difficult to obtain from papers alone. These discussions helped me identify shared technical interests and possible opportunities for future collaboration.

For me, attending an academic conference is about much more than presenting a completed result. It is a chance to test ideas through discussion, learn from experienced researchers, understand how the field is developing, and return to ongoing work with a broader perspective. CPGNSS Forum 2026 was a rewarding experience, and the feedback and connections from the meeting will continue to inform my future research.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

## 从 GNSS 学术共同体中学习

CPGNSS Forum 汇聚了来自高校、科研院所和企业的大量研究人员，会议内容涵盖高精度定位、GNSS 遥感、PPP、RTK、组合导航以及北斗应用等多个方向。整体而言，本次会议的学术水平和组织质量都非常高。无论是大会报告、专题交流还是会间讨论，都让我受益匪浅，也让我了解到许多值得关注的最新研究成果和发展方向。

除了学术交流之外，这次会议也让我见到了许多熟悉的朋友。我遇到了正在南京学习的好友吴煊宇，大家交流了各自近期的研究进展，也聊了不少学习和科研方面的话题。此外，我还与来自长安大学的几位青年同行进行了深入交流，围绕 GNSS-IR、水位监测、低成本 GNSS 设备以及实时算法等共同关注的方向交换了经验和想法。

与研究方向相近的同行面对面讨论，往往能够带来论文阅读之外的新视角。这些交流让我发现了彼此共同关注的技术问题，也为未来可能开展的合作留下了空间。

对我而言，每参加一次学术会议，都不仅仅是一次成果汇报，更是一次学习、检验想法和拓展视野的机会。通过聆听优秀报告、与专家同行交流以及分享自己的研究工作，我相信这些经历会持续推动今后的科研工作。
