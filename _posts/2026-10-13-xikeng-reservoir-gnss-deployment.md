<div data-lang-en markdown="1">

On October 13, 2026, we installed GNSS observation equipment at a water-level station on Xikeng Reservoir. The map below shows the station location, marked GG01.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

2026 年 10 月 13 日，我们有机会在西坑水库的一处水位站安装 GNSS 观测设备。下图标出了水位站所在的位置，点位编号为 GG01。

</div>

<div data-lang-en markdown="1">

The antenna was mounted beside the water-level station overlooking the reservoir. Water levels here are relatively stable, which gives us a chance to see how GNSS observations behave when the water level varies little and to gain experience deploying the equipment in a reservoir environment.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

天线安装在水位站旁，面向库区水面。这里的水位相对稳定，正好可以观察水位变化较小时的 GNSS 观测表现，也为我们积累了在水库环境中部署设备的经验。

</div>
