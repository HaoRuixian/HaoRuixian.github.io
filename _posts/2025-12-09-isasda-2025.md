I delivered an oral presentation titled **“A Sea Surface Height Retrieval Framework Using Multi-Type Observations Based on GNSS Multipath Reflectometry.”**

## A multi-observable GNSS-IR framework

My research has long focused on GNSS-IR-based sea- and water-level monitoring. This work extends conventional approaches that rely mainly on signal-to-noise ratio observations by integrating multiple types of GNSS measurements. The objective is to make fuller use of the reflected-signal information contained in different observables and improve retrieval accuracy, robustness, and applicability under a wider range of observing conditions.

Presenting this framework to researchers working on GNSS remote sensing gave me an opportunity to explain both its methodological motivation and its practical value. The questions and discussions after the talk also helped me see more clearly which parts of the framework should be strengthened in future work.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

## 多类型观测值 GNSS-IR 反演框架

近年来，我一直围绕 GNSS-IR 海（水）面高监测开展研究。本次汇报的工作在传统方法基础上进一步拓展：不再主要依赖信噪比观测值，而是尝试融合多种类型的 GNSS 观测信息，充分挖掘不同观测量中包含的反射信号特征，从而提升海（水）面高反演的精度、稳定性和适用范围。

向从事 GNSS 遥感研究的专家和同行介绍这一框架，让我有机会更系统地说明研究的出发点和实际意义。报告后的提问与讨论，也帮助我进一步明确了后续需要重点完善的环节。

</div>

<div data-lang-en markdown="1">

## My first presentation delivered entirely in English

This symposium marked another personal milestone: it was my first oral presentation at an international academic conference delivered entirely in English. From preparing the slides to giving the talk and answering questions, every stage required me to communicate the research in English. Although I was nervous and could see room for improvement, completing the presentation successfully was an important step in my academic journey.

The experience reminded me that international research communication requires more than reading English papers. It also requires the ability to explain ideas clearly, respond to technical questions, and discuss research with colleagues from different backgrounds. The challenge strengthened my determination to keep improving my academic English and prepare for future international collaboration.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

## 首次完成全英文口头报告

这次会议对我还有一层特殊意义——这是我第一次在国际学术会议上进行全英文口头报告。从 PPT 制作、现场汇报到回答问题，我都使用英语完成。虽然正式汇报时仍然比较紧张，也意识到表达中还有不少可以改进之处，但能够顺利完成这次报告，本身就是我学术成长中的一个重要节点。

这段经历也让我更加深刻地认识到，国际学术交流不仅要求能够阅读英文文献，还需要用英语清晰表达研究思路、回应技术问题，并与不同背景的同行有效讨论。这次挑战增强了我继续提升学术英语能力、为未来国际合作做好准备的决心。

</div>

## Perspectives from ISASDA 2025

During the symposium, I attended presentations on GNSS atmospheric sensing, data assimilation, remote sensing, and high-precision positioning by researchers from China and abroad. Learning about current international developments broadened my perspective and provided new ideas for my own work. Beyond sharing a research result, ISASDA 2025 strengthened my confidence in international academic communication and encouraged me to continue pursuing high-quality GNSS research.

</div>

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

## 从 ISASDA 2025 获得的新视角

会议期间，我聆听了国内外专家学者关于 GNSS 大气探测、数据同化、遥感监测和高精度定位等方向的报告。了解当前国际前沿的发展动态，不仅拓宽了我的研究视野，也为后续工作带来了新的思路。对我而言，ISASDA 2025 不只是一次成果汇报，也增强了我参与国际学术交流的信心，并激励我继续开展高质量的 GNSS 研究。
