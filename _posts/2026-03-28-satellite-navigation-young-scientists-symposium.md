<div data-lang-en markdown="1">

I recently attended the Satellite Navigation 2026 Young Scientists Symposium at Beihang University in Beijing, where I presented a research poster entitled “Near-Real-Time GNSS-IR Sea-Level Retrieval Using Low-Cost GNSS Receivers.” I was honored that the poster received the symposium's Outstanding Poster Award.

## Summarizing a near-real-time GNSS-IR framework

The poster summarized several years of my work on low-cost GNSS-IR sea-level and water-level monitoring. It presented a complete near-real-time processing framework, including the observation platform, raw data acquisition, wireless transmission, sliding-window spectral analysis, and continuous sea-level estimation.

The goal of this work is to demonstrate that affordable GNSS hardware can provide reliable and accurate water-level observations while substantially reducing deployment costs. As low-cost GNSS receivers continue to improve, they are becoming increasingly attractive for environmental monitoring. Compared with high-end geodetic systems, low-cost solutions offer advantages in affordability, deployment flexibility, and the possibility of large-scale installation. The research therefore focuses on how to make the most of these devices while maintaining continuous, accurate, and near-real-time monitoring capability.

## Exchange with young researchers

During the symposium, I discussed GNSS-IR, water-level monitoring, real-time data processing, and low-cost GNSS applications with young researchers and experts from universities and research institutes. These conversations provided valuable feedback on the observation system and processing strategy, while also introducing me to new research directions in satellite navigation and environmental sensing.

According to the organizers, only ten posters were selected for the Outstanding Poster Award. Receiving this recognition was especially meaningful because it acknowledged not only the poster itself, but also the long-term research, engineering work, and repeated field experiments behind it. The award gave me additional confidence to continue developing practical low-cost GNSS-IR monitoring systems.

The symposium once again demonstrated the importance of academic exchange among early-career researchers. Presenting my work, discussing ideas with peers, and learning about recent developments in satellite navigation made this a highly rewarding experience. I look forward to continuing the development of near-real-time GNSS-IR techniques and advancing the practical use of low-cost GNSS systems in hydrological and coastal monitoring.

<div data-lang-zh markdown="1">

近期，我前往北京航空航天大学参加了 Satellite Navigation 2026 青年科学家研讨会（Young Scientists Symposium），并展示了一篇题为《基于低成本 GNSS 设备的近实时 GNSS-IR 海（水）面高反演》的学术海报。很荣幸的是，这项工作最终获得了大会颁发的杰出海报奖（Outstanding Poster Award）。

## 总结近实时 GNSS-IR 研究框架

本次海报主要围绕近年来持续开展的低成本 GNSS-IR 海（水）面高监测研究展开，对整个研究框架进行了系统总结。海报介绍了如何利用低成本 GNSS 接收机构建水位监测系统，以及近实时 GNSS-IR 海（水）面高反演的整体技术路线，包括观测平台搭建、原始数据采集、无线数据传输、滑动窗口谱分析以及连续海（水）面高估计等关键环节。

这项工作的目标是验证，低成本 GNSS 硬件在显著降低建设成本的同时，也能够提供可靠、准确的水位观测结果。近年来，随着低成本 GNSS 接收机性能不断提升，其在环境监测领域展现出了越来越大的应用潜力。与传统高端测量设备相比，低成本方案具有成本低、部署灵活和便于规模化推广等优势。因此，如何充分发挥这些设备的性能，实现连续、高精度、近实时的水位监测，一直是我重点关注的研究方向。本次展示的工作正是这一系列研究成果的阶段性总结。

## 与青年学者交流

会议期间，我与来自不同高校和科研机构的青年学者进行了充分交流，围绕 GNSS-IR、水位监测、实时数据处理以及低成本 GNSS 应用等多个话题展开讨论，也听取了许多专家和同行提出的宝贵建议。这些交流不仅帮助我进一步完善了观测系统和处理方案，也让我了解到当前卫星导航与环境感知领域的一些新方向。

据了解，本届会议仅有 10 篇海报获得杰出海报奖。因此，能够获得这一荣誉，我感到十分荣幸。这不仅是对本项研究工作的认可，也是对长期科研积累、工程实现和多次现场实验的肯定，为我继续开展低成本 GNSS-IR 监测研究增添了更多信心。

通过这次会议，我再次感受到青年学术交流平台的重要意义。无论是展示自己的研究成果，还是学习同行的最新工作，都让我收获颇丰。未来，我希望继续完善近实时 GNSS-IR 海（水）面高监测技术，推动低成本 GNSS 设备在实际水文与海洋监测中的进一步应用。
